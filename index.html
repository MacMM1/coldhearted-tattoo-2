Who We Are

An independent studio, built on trust.

Cold Hearted Tattoo is based in the heart of Brockley Rise, South East London. Founded by tattoo artists Ryan and Alanna, we were created with a simple idea: to build a studio where great tattooing and genuine human connection go hand in hand.

We're a team of artists with different styles, personalities and areas of expertise, but we all share the same passion for creating quality tattoos and making sure every client feels comfortable throughout the experience. We specialise in a wide range of styles, with a strong focus on realism and fine line work — whether you already have a clear idea, want something completely custom, or are looking for inspiration.

About Us

Built by two artists, one vision.

Founder & Artist

Ryan

Ryan's journey into tattooing began at a young age, drawn to the raw, captivating nature of ink. After training and working across different studios, he felt the pull to build something of his own — a space where artistry, professionalism and community could thrive. In 2023, that vision became Cold Hearted.

@ryanr.hart_
Founder & Artist

Alanna

Co-owner and fine line specialist, coming up on her sixth year in the industry. She focuses on clean, detailed designs that are elegant and timeless — with a particular love for micro realism. Whether it's your first tattoo or your fiftieth, she's here to make sure you feel comfortable, heard, and in love with the result.
